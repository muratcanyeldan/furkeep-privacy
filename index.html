Tailkeep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Tailkeep ("the app") is a pet-health tracker that runs entirely on your device. Your privacy is simple to explain because the app does not have a server and does not have an account system.

What we collect

Nothing. Tailkeep does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or pet data. Everything you enter — pets, photos, vaccination records, health events, medications, appointments, reminders, and your profile name — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device.

  • No user accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that send data.
  • No network access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • Your data is never sold, and the app shows no ads.

Permissions we use (and why)

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show local reminders for upcoming vaccines, appointments and medication doses. Reminders are generated and scheduled on the device. The app asks for this permission the first time you create something that needs a reminder, or when you tap "Allow notifications" in Settings.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to re-schedule your pending reminders after the phone reboots, so you don't miss them.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT, added by the Android biometric library): used only for the optional App Lock. Authentication is handled entirely by the Android system — Tailkeep never sees or stores your biometric data.

These permissions are used only for on-device features. They are not used to collect or transmit information.

Device backups (Google)

If you use Android's standard device backup, the operating system may include Tailkeep's data (your pet records, photos and settings) in your device's Google backup, encrypted and managed by Android. This is a transfer you control through your device settings; the developer cannot access it. You can exclude Tailkeep by disabling device backup in your Android settings.

Your data and your control

  • Backup/export: You can export your data (including pet photos) to a file you choose, and import it back. That file is created and stored wherever you decide (e.g. your device or your own cloud drive). Tailkeep never uploads it for you.
  • Sharing: Features like the PDF vaccination card only create a file when you ask for one, and only share it with the app you pick in the share sheet.
  • Retention & deletion: Your data persists until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app — uninstalling permanently deletes all data from your device. Removing a pet hides it in the app (its records are kept locally so a restore can bring them back).

Children

Tailkeep is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone (it collects no data at all).
