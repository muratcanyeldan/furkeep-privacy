Furkeep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Furkeep ("the app") is a pet-health tracker that runs entirely on your device. It is developed by Muratcan Yeldan, an individual developer ("we" in this policy). Your privacy is simple to explain because the app does not have a server and does not have an account system.

What we collect

Nothing. Furkeep does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or pet data. Everything you enter — pets, photos, vaccination records, health events, medications, appointments, reminders, and your profile name — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device.

  • No user accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that send data.
  • No network access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • Your data is never sold, and the app shows no ads.

Permissions we use (and why)

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show local reminders for upcoming vaccines, appointments and medication doses. Reminders are generated and scheduled on the device. The app asks for this permission the first time you create something that needs a reminder, or when you tap "Allow notifications" in Settings.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to re-schedule your pending reminders after the phone reboots, so you don't miss them.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT, added by the Android biometric library): used only for the optional App Lock. Authentication is handled entirely by the Android system — Furkeep never sees or stores your biometric data.

These permissions are used only for on-device features. They are not used to collect or transmit information.

Device backups and transfers (Android)

If you use Android's standard device backup, the operating system may include Furkeep's data (your pet records, photos and settings) in your device's Google backup, encrypted and managed by Android. When you set up a new phone, Android may also copy the same data straight from your old phone (device-to-device transfer). Both are Android features you control in your device settings; the developer cannot access either. You can exclude Furkeep by turning off device backup in your Android settings.

Your data and your control

  • Backup/export: You can export your data (including pet photos) to a file you choose, and import it back. That file is created and stored wherever you decide (e.g. your device or your own cloud drive). Furkeep never uploads it for you.
  • Sharing: Features like the PDF vaccination card only create a file when you ask for one, and only share it with the app you pick in the share sheet.
  • Feedback: "Send feedback" (in About) opens an email draft in your own mail app, addressed to the contact below. The draft includes the app version and your Android version, and you can change or delete anything before sending. We use the emails you send only to reply and to improve the app, and never share them.
  • Retention & deletion: Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a vaccine dose, health event or medication removes it. Removing a pet hides it in the app, but its records stay on the device (and in your exports) so a restore can bring them back. Appointments can be cancelled but not deleted, and past reminders stay in the app's notification list. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app erases all of its data from your device; if Android's device backup is on, reinstalling the app may restore it.

Children

Furkeep is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone (it collects no data at all).
